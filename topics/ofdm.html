OFDM Explained

Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing is a foundation for many modern wireless systems.

Core idea

OFDM distributes information across multiple orthogonal subcarriers. This makes frequency-selective channels easier to manage and supports flexible resource allocation.

Cyclic prefix

A cyclic prefix is inserted before the useful symbol to reduce the impact of multipath-induced inter-symbol interference.
