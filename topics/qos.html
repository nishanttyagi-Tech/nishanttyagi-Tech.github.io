5G QoS

Understand the relationship between QoS flows, 5QI and radio bearers.

Why QoS matters

5G can treat different application traffic differently. QoS flows provide the logical QoS granularity, while radio bearers carry the traffic over the radio interface.

TermSimple meaning
5QIA standardized QoS identifier used to characterize QoS treatment.
QoS FlowA logical unit of traffic with a defined QoS profile.
DRBData Radio Bearer used to carry user-plane traffic over NR.

Validation idea

When testing a QoS requirement, validate the complete chain: application traffic → core QoS policy → QoS flow → radio configuration → measured behaviour.
