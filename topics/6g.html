6G · IMT-2030

6G wireless networks

The next step in wireless: more intelligence, more sensing, more spectrum and tighter integration between communication and computing.

Where 6G stands

6G is still being standardized and studied. ITU calls the future system IMT-2030. The framework is defined in Recommendation M.2160, while 3GPP and other organizations are developing the technical details.

Important: many 6G numbers are targets or research directions, not final commercial specifications. This distinction is important when discussing test plans.

What changes from 5G?

Topic5G today6G direction
AIUsed for selected functions.AI/ML becomes much more deeply integrated into network and air-interface functions.
SensingCommunication is the main job.Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) uses radio infrastructure for sensing.
SpectrumFR1 and FR2 are central.FR3 and higher-frequency/sub-THz research add new capacity options.
PositioningUseful location services.Much more precise positioning is a key target.
IoTBattery-powered device classes are common.Ambient/zero-energy and backscatter devices are being studied.

Six IMT-2030 usage scenarios

  • Immersive Communication
  • Hyper-Reliable and Low-Latency Communication
  • Massive Communication
  • Ubiquitous Connectivity
  • AI and Communication
  • Integrated Sensing and Communication

Why this matters for test engineering

The future test surface becomes broader. In addition to RF, protocol and interoperability testing, engineers will need to think about AI model behaviour, sensing accuracy, positioning, energy efficiency, new spectrum and more distributed architectures.

The accompanying 6G study prepared for this site discusses these topics in greater detail, including protocol architecture, RAN architecture, spectrum, ISAC, AI-native air interface, ambient IoT, NTN and testing implications.
