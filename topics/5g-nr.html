5G NR

5G NR fundamentals

A practical guide to the parts of 5G NR that matter when reading specifications, traces and UE behaviour.

What is 5G NR?

NR means New Radio. It is the radio technology defined by 3GPP for 5G. In practical work, you move between the radio layer, RRC signalling, user-plane protocols and the device/network behaviour seen in logs.

Core areas

AreaSimple view
NumerologyDefines subcarrier spacing and the timing structure used by NR.
Resource gridOrganizes time and frequency resources into resource elements and resource blocks.
Physical channelsCarry control and user data over the radio, such as PDSCH, PUSCH, PDCCH and PUCCH.
RRCControls connection setup, configuration, mobility and many UE/network procedures.
QoSDefines how traffic is treated and mapped toward data radio bearers and the core network.

Why it matters for troubleshooting

When a device fails to connect, drops a session or shows poor performance, the useful question is not only “what failed?” It is “which layer first shows the failure, and what happened immediately before it?” That mindset makes log analysis much faster.

Standards reference: 3GPP TS 38.300 describes NR and NG-RAN overall operation, while TS 38.331 defines the NR RRC protocol.
