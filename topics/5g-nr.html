5G NR

5G NR Fundamentals

Architecture, numerology, resource grid and physical channels.

Architecture

5G NR is the radio access technology used by 5G networks. A useful study path is architecture → numerology → resource grid → channels → signaling procedures.

Numerology and resource grid

NR organizes radio resources in time and frequency. A physical resource block contains 12 consecutive subcarriers, while numerology controls subcarrier spacing and the associated time structure.

Key areas

  • SSB and initial access
  • PRACH
  • PDCCH and DCI
  • PDSCH and PUSCH
  • DM-RS
  • RRC and NAS procedures
