RRC Fundamentals

Radio Resource Control is a key control-plane protocol between the UE and the radio access network.

Why RRC matters

RRC controls radio configuration and supports procedures such as connection establishment, reconfiguration, measurement reporting and mobility-related signaling.

Learning approach

When studying an RRC procedure, trace the messages in sequence and identify what each message configures, requests or confirms.
