6G Fundamentals for Telecom Engineers

A practical introduction to 6G and the evolution from 5G-Advanced toward the ITU IMT-2030 framework. The paper covers 6G vision and standardisation, usage scenarios, capability direction, system architecture, AI-native networks, AI/ML in RAN and network operations, ISAC, spectrum, RIS, NTN, positioning, edge computing, digital twins, network automation, security, privacy, sustainability and research directions.
